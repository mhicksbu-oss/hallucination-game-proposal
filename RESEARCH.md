### Verified contributors

- Char Stiles
- Connie Ye
- Tatyana Mustakos
- Caroline Hermans

Algorat credits the four collectively. Mustakos additionally credits herself as art director and lead artist across the installments. For the first two games, she says she drew all assets and illustrations except the endings and worked on character development, dialogue, reaction-system implementation, and UI design.

### Verified concept and interaction

- Play as a single rat looking for love.
- Meet 21 contestants through conversation.
- Select seven prospects at the beginning.
- Keep or eliminate contestants over successive rounds by giving roses.
- Finish with a proposal to one rat.
- Eliminated contestants receive epilogues; Mustakos states that everyone gets a happy ending.
- Official itch.io tags include `Hand-drawn`, `Dating Sim`, `Funny`, and `Visual Novel`.

### Verified sound credit

The in-game Credits panel states: “Sounds from Youtube Audio Library, Freesound, and mixkit.co.”

### Creator statements relevant to intention

- Connie Ye says Algorat has made work to keep in touch after graduation and for fun.
- Ye says the collective normally makes games for itself and writes dialogue it finds funny.
- In a broader practice interview, Ye describes an interest in delightful, game-adjacent experiences and says she likes to optimize for joy.
- Algorat describes itself as an after-hours passion project that provides free, joyful games.

### Interpretation — not creator claims

- The game compresses reality-dating-show conventions into a short interactive comedy.
- The epilogues soften the cruelty of elimination without removing stakes.
- The handmade visual language makes reality-TV pageantry feel intimate and playful.
- Sound appears to support atmosphere and feedback rather than operate as a separately authored musical work.

## Current working concept

- Working title: **Hallucination**
- Premise: the environment changes whenever the player stops looking at it.
- Interpretation: hallucination as a negotiation between perception, memory, and missing evidence.
- Intended emotional arc: curiosity → doubt → responsibility.
- Player role: observer and accidental author; attention reinforces a version of reality.

All proposal statements above are provisional until the designer confirms them.
